## Final owner-selected capital policy (A, not alternative B)

- Capital less than **100 USDT**: BOOTSTRAP with **at most one** feasible candidate position. Do not assume a minimum lot is executable with 8, 29 or 60 USDT.
- At **100 USDT** settled capital: split **50 USDT protected reserve / 50 USDT active**. Two funded allocation slots, **25 USDT nominal capital per slot**. This is a once-at-activation 50/50 split, **not an ongoing mandatory 50% ratio**.
- Grow the first ladder by **+25 USDT realized net profit** for each additional funded slot. 125=3, 150=4, 175=5, 200=6, 225=7, 250=8, 275=9, 300=10; reserve stays 50 throughout this expansion.
- **Ten slots maximum, not a target to fill**. At 300 USDT: reserve=50, active=250. Next **+50 realized net profit -> reserve** (total 350, reserve 100); next +50 -> active (total 400, active 300 and each of ten nominal budgets rises from 25 to 30); alternate reserve/active 50-USDT chunks indefinitely. A +49.99 chunk is **PENDING**, not an anticipatory slot or reserve deposit.
- **Reverse ladder on losses**: debit **active only**. E.g. 300 total -> R50/A250/10 slots; -40 realized -> R50/A210/**8 slots**. +14 realized -> A224/8; +1 -> A225/9; +25 -> A250/10. Protect R; do not take from R or P to subsidize losses.
- Marked unrealized losses can latch a slot limit **down**. Unrealized profit alone never raises a lowered limit; promote only from **settled net profits** and passing data-health, equity and risk gates. If more open positions exist than allowed slots, prevent new entries and orchestrate **safe later-quote exits**; never fabricate a closing price, force taker liquidation on an unverified book, or silently abandon a pinned position.
- After 10 slots have first been reached, realized profits **first restore the previously funded active rung** after drawdowns before resuming the alternating 50/50 growth steps.
- An adverse tail loss beyond active funds becomes an **explicit deficit / no-entry status**, never a hidden reserve debit. A reserve is **not actually isolated** if it shares Cross Margin collateral; future production segregation requires an independent legally permitted account and explicit owner approval.
- Maintain existing paper controls: 3% drawdown reduces planned per-trade risk, 6% latches HALT requiring review; additional v4 caps **<=0.25% planned loss per entry and <=1% aggregate planned loss relative to effective active trading capital**, subject to stricter legacy controls. Check for policy mismatches with existing `paper_micro` dynamic risk tiers. 25 USDT is an **allocation**, not a maximum loss or a mandatory margin allocation.
- The user's requested **1–2% profit** is an *aspirational net trade return*, never a guarantee or automatic profit event. On 25 USDT at 2% net, **0.50 USDT** per successful trade; +25 USDT would require **50 such winners if no losses**, before any additional round-trip cost assumptions. Independently validate expectancy and fees; enforce NO_TRADE when expected net advantage or feasible liquidity is missing.
- Multiple positions must pass dynamic market-quality, contract minimum, latency, fee, spread, slippage, funding/carry, liquidity and **portfolio correlation** gates. An eligible slot does not imply an entry. Long and short sides will eventually be independent per-symbol paper legs with shared margin/cash accounting; do not enable actual KuCoin Hedge Mode or any real orders.
- Owner-selected operational policy is **Model A / rapid active-rung expansion**. The source also compares Model B / constantly protected 50% as a **research counterfactual only**; you may simulate B separately but must not silently substitute it as the active v4 policy.
- New deposits and external transfers are **not strategy profit**, and cannot automatically unhalt or trigger profit-rung accounting. Explicitly document externally financed initial capital; retain provenance of future capital flows.
